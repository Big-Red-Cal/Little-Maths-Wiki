# Welcome
Thank you for visiting Big Cal's Little Maths Wiki!

## Where am I?
Physically, I don't know. Digitally, you're at Big Cal's Little Maths Wiki.

This resource aims to link you up with the top [[maths people]] and [[resources]] to help you teach maths without having to work it all out yourself.

This is a project that I am building in my own time in an attempt to serve the teaching and learning community. It will never be perfect, but it is all delivered in good faith, in hope that it will help guide you somewhere useful. To that end, I hope that you will forgive any errors on my part in the assembly of this project.

>[!note]
>This is a work in progress. I will try to make additions as frequently as possible, but I am prone to distraction by an array of other personal projects I tinker with.
>
>Check back in from time-to-time to see how it's all coming along.

## Who's Big Cal?
I am!

Though the more detailed version is that I'm an educator working within the further education sector in the UK.

I have a varied academic and work history, but my current role sees me coaching predominately 16-19 year olds to build their confidence and success with their maths. To this end I coach small groups to help them achieve their Functional Skills and GCSE maths qualifications. My role also covers English, Study Skills, and Digital Skills. I have a particular passion for the latter, but my day-to-day is 95% focused on maths support. I started  this role in October 2025 after spending the three years prior teaching carpentry.

As stated above, my background is varied; but to that I would also add, not overtly maths orientated. My A-Levels are in English Language, Sociology and History, with an AS in Computing. My bachelors degree is in History, and after a few years in retail post graduation I attended college to train in carpentry. My interest in computing has seen me work through some online programming courses, but nothing formal. I got a B in GCSE maths (I think), so nothing too showy. My main recollection of GCSE maths is a lot of my teacher doing maths on the board and me not having a clue what most of it meant.

I mention this because I think a lot of people get caught up in "how qualified" educators are in regard to the subjects they teach. I am by no means a specialist, but I am a strong generalist with the view that you can teach anyone anything as long as you have the patience to find the right explanation. This has always helped me to bridge gaps in understanding in the various training roles in which I have served, helping and supporting colleagues and students on their learning journeys, regardless of the current context.

As a generalist, I rely heavily on the work and resources provided by others in order to bring myself up to speed on whatever it is my learners need to learn. As much as I'd love to be able to compose my own personal treasure trove of high quality resources, I think that would be a poor prioritisation of my time in this moment. I think the most valuable thing I can do right now is collect and curate. My goal with this wiki therefore, is to find all the exciting, excellent, existing work that's out there, and assemble this wiki to point you towards it.

So that's who I am and why I'm doing what I'm doing. I hope you find this a valuable resource. If you do, please consider supporting the work of the many fantastic people and organisations this wiki signposts you to.
